Bump mixcr to 4.7.0-395-develop, which bundles gene library v6.5. The library adds IGK and IGL light chains for Macaca mulatta, so IG runs on that species no longer fail at the light-chain export. The update also adds IGK and IGL for Macaca fascicularis and IGH, IGK and IGL for rat, and it raises the maximum number of V hits kept by amplicon presets from 2 to 15.
